Personal use

Silverside is for archival copies of discs that you own, for your personal use. It does not support piracy: do not use it to copy discs that are not yours, or to share copies with other people. Follow the copyright law where you live. You use Silverside at your own risk: it comes with no warranty. The app asks you to agree to this before its first rip.
